- **fix(a11y):** the light theme's primary button painted white text on a gradient whose violet end measured **3.96:1** — under WCAG AA's 4.5:1 for normal text, on the most-clicked control in the product. The crimson end was fine at 5.88:1, which is why it was never noticed: axe reports a gradient backdrop as `incomplete` rather than as a violation, so the production scan (axe-core 4.13.0) never measured it. `--grad-brand` now ends at `--color-accent-on-primary`, a darkened violet (`#9333ea`) that takes the worst point of the fill to **5.16:1**. The brand accent `--color-accent-light` is unchanged — it stays the brand colour everywhere it is decoration rather than a backdrop for text — and the dark theme, which writes black on the gradient and already cleared AA (5.31:1), points `--color-accent-on-primary` straight back at it. A new test computes the WCAG ratio from `globals.css` at 21 points along the gradient in both themes, so this is measured rather than asserted as a hex; restoring the old violet makes it fail at 3.96:1.
