- **fix(openapi):** two endpoints were documented twice under different template variable names (`…/agents/{agentId}/dns` beside `…/agents/{id}/dns`, and the same pair for `/mappings`). OpenAPI 3.x treats those as the same endpoint, so the document was invalid and `oasdiff` refused to diff it — meaning `check:openapi-breaking` had been falling through to its graceful skip and reporting a green that measured nothing, on every PR. The blocks are merged under the route's real parameter name, keeping the hand-written schemas and the `x-loopback-only` annotation the generated stubs were holding. `check:openapi-routes` now reports paths that collapse to the same endpoint, so it cannot come back ([#62](https://github.com/LMPrado-DZ23/OmniRoute/pull/62))
